# TruLura Guiding Principles

_A one-page synthesis of the six documents in this folder — every line below is a distillation of something stated directly in the Blueprint, not a new claim. Read this first; read the Product, Engineering, AI, Design, and Documentation Constitutions for the full grounding and section citations behind each line._

## The One-Sentence Version

**TruLura is built to be a platform users trust rather than a platform that competes for their attention** (§25.18) — every principle below exists to protect that distinction.

## Ten Guiding Principles

1. **Safety and consent outrank everything else, structurally.** When systems conflict, Safety & Consent wins, then State & Mode Logic, then AI Interpretation, then Experience Systems (Feed, Matchmaking, Creator Tools) — in that order, always (§19.1.1).

2. **Emotional well-being outranks engagement.** Every system that could be tuned to maximize raw engagement is instead built to prioritize "user well-being, emotional alignment, and healthy interaction patterns" (§4), including AI ("emotional well-being over raw engagement," §11.1.1), monetization, and gamification.

3. **Identity is one continuous thing, not fragments.** Each user has a single master identity that adapts by context — modes and states change what that identity can do, not who it is (§1.1, §2).

4. **AI assists; it never decides alone.** AI may interpret, guide, and inform timing — it does not control monetization, does not override platform systems, and does not replace human judgment or connection (§11, §11.14, §14.14, §15.4).

5. **MoodSync is the one emotional source of truth.** Every system that needs emotional context consumes MoodSync's signals; none maintains its own parallel model of a user's emotional state (§11.2.3, §12).

6. **The interface must never manufacture urgency, dependency, or confusion.** Not to increase engagement, not for any reason — this is stated as an absolute, not a tradeoff (§20.20.2, §25.18).

7. **Monetization serves the platform's values; the platform never serves monetization.** Revenue generation "does not override" safety, trust, or user well-being, and the creator economy is deliberately non-viral (§7, §7.1.1, §7.9).

8. **Systems own what they define and consume what they don't.** A system that only consumes another system's output (e.g., Vent Space consuming MoodSync) must never re-implement or fork that owned behavior locally (§14, §15, and the "owns / does not own" pattern repeated across Sections 12–17).

9. **When something is undecided, it gets logged — never guessed.** Every open question in the Blueprint's own history was resolved as a mechanical fix, a content-preserving merge, or an explicitly logged decision sent to the right owner (Product, Legal, or Trust & Safety) — never quietly assumed (Project Completion Summary).

10. **Product Decisions live in exactly one place.** No downstream document — architecture, engineering, design, or business — is permitted to author its own competing product decision. If a gap needs one, it's logged in the Product Decisions Register and referenced from wherever it was found.

11. **Context always outweighs popularity.** Discovery, recommendations, compatibility, communities, creators, and AI should prioritize contextual relevance, emotional alignment, intent, safety, and relationship quality over raw popularity, engagement metrics, or virality. This principle underpins Discovery, MoodSync, Creator Systems, Communities, AI Intelligence, and Feed Ranking.

## How These Apply Going Forward

These ten principles are the standard every future product, engineering, AI, design, and documentation decision should be checked against — not a checklist to satisfy once, but the philosophy every subsequent document in this system inherits from and must remain consistent with, per the Documentation Constitution's authority order.
