# TruLura AI Constitution

*Extracted from Blueprint Sections 11 (AI Intelligence), 12 (MoodSync), and 15 (AI Companion). Covers only the Blueprint's stated boundaries and philosophy for AI behavior — not the AI decision layer's implementation, signal set, or parameters (those are open Engineering Gaps: EG-05 for MoodSync's signal set, and belong in `docs/07-AI` once specified).*

## What AI Is For

"The AI layer exists to improve clarity, personalization, pacing, safety, and relational quality across the platform. It is not designed to maximize raw engagement or exploit emotional behavior" (§11.1).

## The Five Core Intelligence Principles (§11.1.1)

1. Emotional well-being over raw engagement
2. User intent alignment over algorithmic control
3. Safety and consent as primary constraints
4. Adaptive learning without manipulation
5. Transparency and user awareness

## What AI Must Never Do

Stated repeatedly and consistently across every section that touches AI:

- **Never control monetization** — "While the AI system does not control monetization, it influences when monetization features are presented" (§11.14). Monetization enhances interaction; AI may only inform its timing, never decide its outcome.
- **Never override or replace platform systems** — "The AI System does not replace or override platform systems" (§11.14.1). "AI intelligence enhances MoodSync outputs but does not replace or override the foundational emotional systems that govern platform adaptation" (§12.11.1).
- **Never replace human connection or judgment** — "The system is designed to assist, interpret, and guide — not manipulate or replace human judgment" (§11 introduction). "Vent Space is supported by the AI system but does not replace human connection" (§14.14). The AI Companion explicitly "does not replace human relationships" (§15.4).
- **Never misrepresent its own decisions** — misrepresenting "AI decisions, recommendations, or platform behavior" is on the platform-wide "must never" list (§25.18), and applies to AI specifically as much as to any other system.

## AI Consumes Signals, It Does Not Own Emotional State

MoodSync (§12) is the system of record for emotional state; the AI layer "consumes contextual signals produced by MoodSync and translates them into actionable intelligence" (§11.2.3) — it does not generate emotional states itself, and per §11.14.1 it "consumes emotional intelligence signals generated by MoodSync but does not own or define those systems." Any AI feature that needs mood/emotional context must read from MoodSync's model, not maintain its own parallel one.

## No Dependency Loops

The AI Companion's canonical boundary language (preserved during the Blueprint's canonical merge, per the Project Completion Summary) establishes that AI Companion must not create circular ownership with the systems it depends on (AI Intelligence, MoodSync) — a one-directional consumption relationship, not a loop.
